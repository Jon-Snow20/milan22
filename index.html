            Before you travel

            Questions, answered.

            Who can participate?

            Any enrolled UG or PG student with a valid college ID. Teams are drawn from a single college. Alumni and school students can attend pro nights but cannot enter competitions.

            When do registrations close?

            Friday 27 November at 11:59 PM. Team lists lock then, with one substitution allowed until 2 December.

            Is accommodation provided?

            The Stay Pass includes three nights in campus hostels, with separate blocks and 600 beds in total. Rooms are allotted in the order passes are bought.

            How does travel reimbursement work?

            Finalists claim up to ₹2,500 at the Gate 2 helpdesk after the prize ceremony, with original tickets and the college ID.

            What about safety and access?

            There is a medical tent at the Main Stage, a quiet room in the Library, step-free routes to every venue and a 24-hour helpline printed on every badge.

            Can I get a refund?

            Passes are refundable in full until 20 November, and half until 30 November. After that they can be transferred to another student of your college.

            See you at the quad.

            SRM, Chennai. Gates open Friday 4 December at 9:00 AM. Registration desk at Gate 2 opens at 8:00 AM.
